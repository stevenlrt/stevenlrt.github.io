Our Story

Rooted in a quiet corner

Stillwell opened in 2021 in a converted corner storefront, built on one idea: yoga doesn't need to be loud, trendy, or crowded to change how you move through your day.

We kept our class sizes small on purpose. Every teacher here knows your name, your injuries, and where you're holding tension before you say a word.
