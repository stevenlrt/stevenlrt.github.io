Who we are

Modforge Retro started in a spare bedroom, soldering iron in hand, bringing dead PS3s and forgotten iPods back to life. What began as a hobby turned into a shop built on one rule: every unit gets tested before it ships.

We specialize in shell swaps, internal upgrades, capacitor replacements, and full restorations for hardware most repair shops won't touch anymore.
